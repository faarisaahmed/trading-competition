News Hound

“The tape tells you what happened. The wire tells you why.”

Event-driven. Scores the Alpaca news wire with a finance-tuned sentiment lexicon (no LLM -- a deterministic, checked-in dictionary plus negation and intensifier handling), decays by age, weights by source and by how exclusively the story is about that ticker, then requires price confirmation before buying. Exits on sentiment decay or a time stop.

Q-Learner

#2 +1.40%

“It doesn't know what a stock is. It knows what worked.”

Tabular Q-learning over a discretised state (trend bucket, dislocation bucket, volatility regime, current position) choosing among flat / half / full exposure. Reward is ATR-normalised P&L minus a turnover penalty. Pre-trained offline on historical bars, then keeps learning online with a decaying epsilon. Its picker is a LinUCB contextual bandit -- same "learn from reward" philosophy, applied to universe selection.

The Gambler

#3 +1.13%

“Kelly said bet the edge. Kelly never said how big the edge was.”

Deliberately high-variance. Estimates a win probability and payoff ratio from the recent tape, sizes with a *fractional Kelly multiplied up*, and concentrates. After a loss it steps size up (bounded, 3 rungs); after a win it resets. Prefers lottery-like names: high vol, wide range, gappy. Exists to answer "does variance-seeking beat discipline over 3 weeks?"

Stat Arb

#8 -0.65%

“I don't care where the market goes. I care where the spread goes.”

Relative value. Finds pairs with a stationary spread (correlation, OLS hedge ratio, ADF t-stat, half-life), then trades the spread's z-score. Because the competition is long-only, it expresses the pair as a *rotation*: hold the cheap leg, switch when the spread flips, flat when the spread is fair. Market-neutral in spirit, cash-account in practice.
